# Project Specification: Parishisht Construction Private Limited

## Executive Summary
Parishisht Construction Private Limited is a specialized EPC and turnkey public works contracting company in Bihar, India. The company undertakes high-impact civil and public health engineering projects, notably for the Public Health Engineering Department (PHED), Bihar Urban Infrastructure Development Corporation (BUIDCO), Road Construction Department (RCD), and Rural Works Department (RWD).

## Core Deliverables
1. **Wix Master Content Document:** Ready-to-paste text blocks and visual layout instructions for the complete corporate website.
2. **Specialized Sections:**
   - Dedicated "Investors & Financial Strength" module emphasizing solvency, banking consortiums, and JV qualifications.
   - Dedicated "Careers & Leadership" module for recruiting top civil engineers, project managers, and quality testing personnel in Bihar.
   - 4-Sector Capability Showcase (PHE, Civil Infrastructure, Urban Drainage, Heavy Works).
3. **Interactive Web Portal Prototype:** A fully responsive, standalone HTML5/Tailwind/Lucide web portal with interactive tabs, project showcases, recruitment modal, and tender inquiry desk.
4. **Brand Design System:** High-end public sector color palette, typography hierarchy, and asset specifications.
