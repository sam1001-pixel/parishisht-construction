# Brand Identity & Design System: Parishisht Construction Private Limited

## 1. Brand Essence & Tone of Voice
- **Archetype:** The Sovereign Builder / Civic Pillar.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, robust, technically precise, accountable, and rooted in Bihar's progress.
- **Audience:** Government Chief Engineers, Tender Evaluation Committees, PSU Executives, Bank Managers, Engineering Recruits.
- **Keywords:** Structural Integrity, Public Health Engineering, EPC Execution, Jal Jeevan Mission, Bankability, Zero Fatality.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Primary Heading Font:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Font weights: Bold 700 / Semi-Bold 600)
- **Body & Technical Specs Font:** `Inter` (Font weights: Regular 400 / Medium 500)
- **Monospace / Metric Counters:** `JetBrains Mono` or tabular figures for currency and quantities.

### Type Scale:
- **Hero H1:** 48px - 60px / Line-height 1.1 / Bold
- **Section H2:** 32px - 40px / Line-height 1.2 / Semi-Bold
- **Card H3:** 20px - 24px / Line-height 1.3 / Semi-Bold
- **Subheadings / Lead Paragraph:** 18px - 20px / Line-height 1.5 / Regular
- **Body Text:** 15px - 16px / Line-height 1.6 / Regular
- **Meta / Pills / Badges:** 12px - 13px / Uppercase / Tracking-wider / Semi-Bold

---

## 4. Visual Imagery & Photography Standards
1. **Infrastructure Scale:** Authentic photography of concrete batching, heavy pipe-laying (DI pipes), water treatment structures, and asphalt paving.
2. **Human Safety:** Personnel must always be depicted wearing proper PPE (helmets, high-visibility reflective vests, safety boots).
3. **Regional Realism:** Showcase authentic landscapes of Bihar (Ganges riverine projects, rural pipeline networks, urban drainage works in Patna/Gaya).
4. **Accreditations:** High-resolution vector badges for ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and Government of Bihar empanelment.
