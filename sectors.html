Piped Drinking Water Grids & Jal Jeevan Mission

Parishisht Construction specializes in rural and urban piped drinking water transmission grids. We execute intake wells in alluvial Ganga riverbeds, high-capacity Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Over Head Service Reservoirs (OHSR) up to 20-meter staging, and extensive DI K7/K9 and PE-100 HDPE distribution networks.

Executing major state highway corridors and arterial bypasses adhering to IRC and MoRTH specifications. We handle flexible asphalt pavements, rigid concrete pavements (PQC with DLC sub-base), subgrade stabilization, and flood-resilient embankment works.

Key Technical Scope & Capabilities

  • Rigid Pavement (PQC): Automated slipform paving with electronic level sensors ensuring superior ride quality and 30-year design life.
  • Flexible Bituminous Surfacing: High-density DBM and BC laying with computerized hot mix plants and multi-wheel pneumatic rollers.
  • Subgrade & Geosynthetics: Fly ash and lime stabilization over expansive black cotton and alluvial clays with non-woven geotextile separation layers.
  • Cross Drainage Structures: Precast and cast-in-situ RCC box culverts designed to withstand 1-in-50-year Bihar monsoon flood discharges.
