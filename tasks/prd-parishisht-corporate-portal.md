# PRD: Parishisht Construction Private Limited Corporate Web Portal

## 1. Introduction/Overview
Parishisht Construction Private Limited is an empaneled public sector EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractor based in Bihar, India. The company specializes in large-scale Civil Infrastructure, Public Health Engineering (PHE / Jal Jeevan Mission), Municipal Works (BUIDCO), and Government Contracting. This project delivers a comprehensive corporate web portal and ready-to-paste content architecture for Wix and custom web stacks to establish authority with government tender boards, institutional investors, joint-venture partners, and engineering recruits.

## 2. Goals
- Deliver authoritative corporate copywriting and content blocks tailored specifically for public sector construction in Bihar.
- Position the company as a Tier-1 bankable EPC contractor with high solvency, proven equipment fleets, and unblemished execution records.
- Establish dedicated, high-conversion sections for "Investors & Financial Strength" and "Careers & Leadership".
- Provide a ready-to-paste Wix / Wix Studio layout blueprint with exact block definitions and styling parameters.
- Provide a functional, responsive, high-fidelity web prototype with interactive sector filters, Bihar district project maps, and recruitment modals.

## 4. Functional Requirements
- **FR-1:** Portal must feature high-impact B2G messaging highlighting Bihar state departments (PHED, BUIDCO, RCD, RWD).
- **FR-2:** Portal must showcase live trust metrics: ₹250+ Cr. aggregate works, 15+ Bihar districts, 100% on-time milestone delivery.
- **FR-3:** Sector grid must feature 4 specialized engineering pillars with technical specifications.
- **FR-4:** Investors section must detail banking consortium tie-ups, solvency certificates, and Joint Venture capabilities.
- **FR-5:** Careers section must allow job seekers to browse open positions by Bihar district and launch an application dialog.
- **FR-6:** Contact & Tendering desk must provide dedicated email routing for tenders, vendors, and general queries.

## 5. Non-Goals
- No backend payment gateway integration (public sector tenders operate through official government e-Procurement portals).
- No authenticated client portal requiring database login for Phase 1.

## 6. Technical Considerations
- Zero-dependency standalone HTML5 bundle with CDN Tailwind CSS v3/v4 and Lucide Icons for instant, lightweight offline/online viewing.
- Fast Core Web Vitals (< 1.2s First Contentful Paint).
- Mobile-first touch responsiveness for government officials reviewing on smartphones.

## 7. Success Metrics
- 100% complete Wix-compatible copy for all 8 main sections.
- Verified pre-qualification compliance for Bihar public sector tenders.
- Mobile responsiveness score 100 on desktop and mobile viewports.
